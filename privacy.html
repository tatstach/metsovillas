Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Who we are

This website, metsovillas.gr, is operated by MetsoVillas, four private guest suites in Metsovo, Greece. For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at info@metsovillas.gr or +30 694 690 5096, or by post at Vasileiou Mpousvarou 2, Metsovo 442 00, Greece.

What this site does not do

This site has no booking form and no user accounts. All reservations are made directly by phone or email. We do not collect names, payment details, or booking information through the website itself.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use a cookie consent banner and, if you accept it, Google Analytics. Your choice (accept or decline) is remembered on your device using your browser's local storage, so we don't ask you again on every visit. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no data is sent to Google Analytics.

Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics (provided by Google Ireland Limited) collects anonymized information about how visitors use this site: pages viewed, time spent, approximate location (from IP address, which we ask Google to anonymize), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This helps us understand which pages are useful and improve the site. Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months and then automatically deleted.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser's site data for metsovillas.gr, or by using your browser's cookie settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using Google's browser add-on.

Google Maps

Our Contact page embeds a Google Maps location. When that map loads, Google may set its own cookies and collect data such as your IP address, independently of your analytics choice, under Google's own privacy policy.

Legal basis

We only load Google Analytics after you actively consent, as required under the ePrivacy rules and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You are always free to decline or to withdraw consent later, and doing so has no effect on your ability to contact us or view the site.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data (if any) we or our processors hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete that data
  • Object to or restrict how it is processed
  • Withdraw consent to analytics at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) if you believe your data has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@metsovillas.gr.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a new tool to the site. The date below shows when it was last updated.
